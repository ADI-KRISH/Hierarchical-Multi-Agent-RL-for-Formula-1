How these numbers are made

Baseline driver. Before the lap it plans one speed profile for a derated car: corners at a fraction of the car's lateral grip and braking points as if the brakes had only part of their capacity. Then it follows that plan, steering back to the racing line when it runs wide. Each episode draws its margins from a seeded jitter, so the 20 laps differ but replay exactly.

Limit lap. The same planner with no margins, from a standing start. No driver in this simulator can lap faster, so it is the ceiling for the RL agent too.

Simulator. Point-mass car on a track of constant-curvature segments, 50 Hz control. Car figures are the cited EXAMPLE_CAR values in src/f1rl/config.py. Both tracks here are synthetic layouts; real circuits load from FastF1 data.
